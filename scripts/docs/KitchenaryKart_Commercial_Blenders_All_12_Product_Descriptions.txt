[Heading1] Catalogue corrections
[] For the catalogue team. Keep these corrections separate from the product descriptions.
[Heading2] Model names
[] The website headings HE750WB, HE750WC and HE750WE correspond to SKUs ending HM750WB, HM750WC and HM750WE. The supplied nameplates and model video use HM. Update those three website headings to match the nameplates.
[Heading2] Blade and whisk configurations
[] The supplied model videos identify WB as the 450 mm blade version, WC as the 530 mm blade version and WE as the whisk version for both 750 W families. Some loose BLM photos show a different attachment fitted to the labelled motor body. Use the correct assembled model photo for each listing. The BLM video frame in this document is a family reference, not a photograph of three items included in one purchase.
[Heading2] Countertop blender power
[] The red and black 2 L listings state 4500 W. Check the machine nameplate to establish whether this is rated input power or a peak claim before adding it to the revised description or SEO title.
[Heading2] Drink mixer cup capacity
[] The single drink mixer specification panel says 750 ml; gallery image 2 says 700 ml. Confirm the supplied cup capacity before publishing a numeric capacity. The gallery and specification panel also show different weights.
[Heading2] Weights and speed figures
[] Several immersion blender weights differ between the website, supplied labels and video motor-head figures. Verify complete-unit net weight separately from motor-head weight. For whisk models, motor rpm must not be presented as whisk attachment rpm without confirming the attachment gearing.
[Heading2] Hand mixer product name
[] The 500 W listing is titled Electric Hand Blender, but its gallery shows a handheld mixer with twin beaters. The proposed title and copy describe the pictured mixer. Match the title and photos to the item supplied.
[Heading2] Claims for the sound enclosure
[] Use soundproof blender as the product search term, while describing the cover as helping reduce noise. Do not claim silent operation, a decibel reduction, a safety interlock or unbreakable glass without supporting specifications.
