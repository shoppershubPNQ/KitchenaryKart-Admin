[Heading2] Catalogue capacities versus supplied labels
[] KKHE0085-FY8L21 and KKHE0086-FY8L31 are titled 8L per section, but supplied graphics state 6L per section. KKHE0088-FY83 is titled 16L but its graphic states 6L + 6L. KKHE0093-FY82 and KKHE0095-FY81 are also titled 8L per tank while their supplied plates state 6L. The FY81H photo for KKHE0101/0102 shows 6L against an 8L catalogue name. Existing product names are retained for matching; whether these differences represent gross/net volume or a listing error is not established. Confirm the correct capacity and fill limits before publishing capacity claims. The copy does not call these tanks 8L oil-fill capacity.
[Heading2] Digital standing fryer power
[] KKHE0097-FY14LD and KKHE0098-FY28LS list 1,800 W on the website. The supplied nameplates clearly state 18 kW, 380 V, 50 Hz, with matching tank layouts. The descriptions use the supplied nameplate ratings. Verify the current delivered model and correct the website specification field before publication.
[Heading2] Per-head versus total power
[] The website specification cards often show only one head’s power. Descriptive text and supplied labels support 2,500 W × 2 for KKHE0085, KKHE0088 and KKHE0093; 2,500 W × 3 for KKHE0086; and 2,000 W × 2 for KKHE0091. KKHE0087 has no clear total-load confirmation, so its copy omits a wattage claim.
