The website calls 818 and 820 Eco models; these labels are retained only to identify the existing listings. No new photo was assigned to 818H, 820H or gas model 720.
[Heading2] 818 temperature and weight
[] The 818 graphic states 50–300°C in the feature strip but room temperature to 180°C in its specification table. Its copy therefore says adjustable temperature without a numerical range. The updated graphic lists 15.5 kg, while the 818 webpage lists 18 kg; weight is omitted from the 818 description. Confirm both values from the current machine nameplate and manual. The 818H webpage separately lists 15.5 kg; matching weight alone does not establish that the photo belongs to 818H.
[Heading2] Heating area and external dimensions
[] For 820 and 822, the images state a 29 × 21 inch heating area, equivalent to approximately 736.6 × 533.4 mm, but an overall body of 730 × 450 × 230 mm. The claimed plate is larger than the stated body footprint. The 818 image similarly shows approximately 14 × 21 inches for the plate but 514 × 420 × 193 mm overall. Heating-area measurements are omitted from the three updated descriptions pending physical measurement. The 730 × 450 × 230 mm body size is retained as the stated outline size for 820 and 822, not as a cooking-area measurement.
